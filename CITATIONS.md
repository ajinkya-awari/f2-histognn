# Scientific and software citations

This repository contains no accepted real-data benchmark result. The following
works define its graph methods and approved real-data smoke dependencies.

- Kipf, T. N., & Welling, M. (2017). Semi-Supervised Classification with Graph Convolutional Networks. *ICLR*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.02907
- Hamilton, W. L., Ying, R., & Leskovec, J. (2017). Inductive Representation Learning on Large Graphs. *NeurIPS*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.02216
- Velickovic, P. et al. (2018). Graph Attention Networks. *ICLR*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.10903
- Rampasek, L. et al. (2022). Recipe for a General, Powerful, Scalable Graph Transformer. *NeurIPS*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12454
- Fey, M., & Lenssen, J. E. (2019). Fast Graph Representation Learning with PyTorch Geometric. https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.02428
- The Cancer Genome Atlas Research Network. TCGA data portal and acknowledgement guidance. https://www.cancer.gov/tcga
- Vu, Q. D. et al. (2019). HoVer-Net: Simultaneous segmentation and classification of nuclei in multi-tissue histology images. *Medical Image Analysis*, 58, 101563. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2019.101563
- Gamper, J. et al. (2020). PanNuke Dataset Extension, Insights and Baselines. https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.10778

The approved real-data smoke pins the exact HoVer-Net revision and checkpoint
hash in the runbook. Any benchmark publication must also record the exact GDC
query/release, manifest hash, cohort, split, seeds, and generated-artifact
hashes.
